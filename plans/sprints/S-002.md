# S-002: Staff and admin authentication

**Status:** Done  **Start:** 2026-11-02  **End:** 2026-11-13  **Milestone:** none

## Goal
Clinic staff and admins sign in with a username and password, every clinic route is protected by role, and the shared admin token is gone. Approved on 2026-10-27.

## Scope
T-019, T-020, T-021, T-022, T-023 (feature: `plans/features/authentication/`), plus T-028 (Render test hosting, `plans/features/render-deployment/`; added 2026-10-28 by decision of the maintainers), T-029, T-030 and T-031 (hooks and CI tooling, `plans/features/hooks-approval-gates/`; added 2026-10-28 by decision of the maintainers, to finish before the v0.1.0 release). Decisions: ADR-0001 to ADR-0003, ADR-0005, DDR-0001 to DDR-0003.

## Out of scope
Patient and doctor accounts, SSO, refresh tokens, email reset, MFA, audit log UI, migrating the frontend off the single page (ADR-0001).

## Capacity
Up to four parallel dev sessions while wave 2 and T-022 overlap; each ticket needs a human review before merge. Estimates: T-019 M, T-020 S, T-021 M, T-022 M, T-023 S. No hours were given, so capacity is judged by parallelism and review, not hours.

## Risks
- T-019 is the bottleneck for everything else.
- T-020, T-021 and T-023 all touch `app/routes.py`, `app/models.py` and `app/store.py`; expect merge conflicts and rebase early.
- T-022 edits `app/static/index.html` while T-021 removes the admin-token field on Revenue; whoever merges second rebases.
- T-022 starts against the contract, so any API change in waves 1-2 must be copied back to the spec and the Artifacts.
- The seed admin needs `AUTH_SECRET`, `ADMIN_USERNAME` and `ADMIN_PASSWORD` before first start on any deployed site.
